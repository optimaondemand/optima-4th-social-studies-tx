CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 1 Assessment — Texas Foundations: Identity, Freedom, and Geography
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.7A–B · 4.8A–C · 4.11A–B · 4.13C · 4.19A–G · 4.21A–D
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. A diary written by a Texas settler in 1836 is an example of a...
*a) primary source
b) secondary source
c) map legend
d) cultural region

2. Which of these is a SECONDARY source?
a) A letter written by Stephen F. Austin
b) A photograph taken in 1901
*c) A textbook chapter written today about the Texas Revolution
d) A hand-drawn map from 1840

3. What is the FIRST thing a good historian does when studying a source?
a) Decide whether they like it
*b) Observe and describe what they actually see
c) Throw out anything confusing
d) Write the conclusion

4. Why should historians ask cautionary questions about a source?
a) To prove the source is useless
*b) Because a source might leave things out or show only one point of view
c) Because primary sources are always wrong
d) To make the work take longer

5. What does the Declaration of Independence explain?
*a) Why people have the right to govern themselves and to break from an unfair government
b) How to build roads
c) The names of all Texas counties
d) How to elect a governor of Texas

6. What does the Bill of Rights do?
a) Creates the three branches of government
*b) Protects individual freedoms, like speech and religion
c) Lists the four regions of Texas
d) Sets the price of goods

7. What does "consent of the governed" mean?
a) The king decides everything.
*b) Government gets its power from the agreement of the people.
c) Only soldiers can vote.
d) Laws do not apply to leaders.

8. Which list names the four physical regions of Texas?
a) Gulf, Plains, Puebloan, Southeastern
*b) Coastal Plains, North Central Plains, Great Plains, Mountains and Basins
c) Panhandle, Hill Country, Piney Woods, Big Bend
d) North, South, East, West

9. Which region is the largest and has the most people?
*a) Coastal Plains
b) North Central Plains
c) Great Plains
d) Mountains and Basins

10. Which region is the smallest and driest, in far west Texas?
a) Coastal Plains
b) North Central Plains
c) Great Plains
*d) Mountains and Basins

11. What does a map legend (or key) do?
a) Shows which direction is north
*b) Explains what the colors and symbols on the map mean
c) Tells who drew the map
d) Measures the temperature

12. Why did many early Texas settlements grow near rivers?
a) Rivers kept people away from farms.
*b) Rivers provided water for drinking and farming, and a way to travel and trade.
c) Rivers were the only places with mountains.
d) The law required it.

SHORT ANSWER

13. Describe the Source Study routine: observe, classify, question, claim. Use one example source (a photo, letter, map, or diary) to show what you would do at each step.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names all four steps in order AND applies them accurately to a specific source (e.g., describes details, labels it primary or secondary, asks what it leaves out, makes an evidence-based claim). 1 pt — Names the steps but gives only a partial or vague example. 0 pts — Inaccurate or no response.]

14. Choose one physical region of Texas. Describe its land and climate, and explain how its geography affects where people live or what work they do.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Accurately describes the region (e.g., Mountains and Basins: dry, mountains and desert; Coastal Plains: flat, wet, near the Gulf) AND explains a clear effect on settlement or work (fewer people, ranching, ports, farming, cities). 1 pt — Accurate description OR effect, but not both. 0 pts — Inaccurate.]

15. Give one example of people modifying the environment in Texas (such as building a dam, digging a canal, or building the Houston Ship Channel). Explain one benefit and one possible cost.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Gives an accurate modification AND names both a benefit (water, flood control, trade, farmland) and a cost (lost habitat, changed rivers, expense, displaced people). 1 pt — Example with only a benefit or only a cost. 0 pts — No accurate example.]
