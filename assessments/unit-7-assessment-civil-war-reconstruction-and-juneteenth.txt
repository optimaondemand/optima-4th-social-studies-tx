CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 7 Assessment — Civil War, Reconstruction, and Juneteenth
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.4A · 4.14D · 4.19A,B,C,E,F · 4.21E
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. According to the Texas secession convention's own written reasons in 1861, what was the main cause of Texas leaving the United States?
a) A border argument with Mexico
*b) Protecting slavery
c) High taxes on cattle
d) A fight over the capital city

2. What did Governor Sam Houston do in March 1861?
a) He led Texas soldiers into battle in Virginia.
*b) He refused to take an oath to the Confederacy and was removed from office.
c) He signed the order of secession.
d) He moved the capital to Galveston.

3. The 1861 secession vote map shows that...
*a) most Texas counties voted to secede, but some, including counties in the Hill Country and North Texas, voted no.
b) every Texan voted the same way.
c) most Texas counties voted to stay in the United States.
d) only cities were allowed to vote.

4. Why did goods like coffee, salt, and cloth run short in Texas during the war?
a) Texans stopped buying them.
*b) Union ships blockaded Texas ports.
c) Mexico refused to trade with Texas.
d) The goods were sent to the army in Mexico.

5. Women running farms and businesses while men were away in the army is an example of what kind of effect?
a) Political
b) Economic only
*c) Social
d) Military

6. Why did Colorado County print its own paper money in 1862?
*a) Coins and U.S. money were scarce during the war.
b) The county wanted to leave Texas.
c) The Union army ordered it.
d) Paper money was cheaper than food.

7. Which battle near Brownsville in May 1865 was the last land battle of the Civil War?
a) Sabine Pass
b) Galveston
*c) Palmito Ranch
d) San Jacinto

8. What did General Orders No. 3 announce in Galveston on June 19, 1865?
a) That Texas had won the Civil War
*b) That all enslaved people in Texas were free
c) That Texas was rejoining Mexico
d) That the capital would move to Austin

9. Why was freedom announced in Texas about two and a half years after the Emancipation Proclamation?
a) Texas had already ended slavery.
*b) Texas was far from Union armies, so the order could not be enforced until Union troops arrived in 1865.
c) The Emancipation Proclamation did not mention any states.
d) Texans voted to wait.

10. What were the Black Codes passed by Texas lawmakers in 1866?
a) Laws that gave Black Texans land
*b) Laws that kept Black Texans from voting, holding office, or serving on juries
c) Rules for Freedmen's Bureau schools
d) The words of General Orders No. 3

11. Which of these happened during Reconstruction in Texas?
a) Texas left the United States.
*b) Black Texans such as George T. Ruby and Matthew Gaines were elected to the Texas Senate.
c) Slavery became legal again.
d) Juneteenth became a national holiday.

12. In what year did Texas become the first state to make Juneteenth an official holiday?
a) 1866
b) 1872
*c) 1980
d) 2021

SHORT ANSWER

13. How can one war affect Texans differently at the same time? Compare how the Civil War affected two different groups (for example: soldiers' families, women, enslaved Texans, Unionists, Tejanos, frontier communities).
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names two groups AND gives an accurate, specific effect for each, showing how the experiences differed. 1 pt — Names two groups with a vague effect, or one group with a specific effect. 0 pts — No specific groups or effects.]

14. General Orders No. 3 promised "an absolute equality of personal rights." Explain what really happened after 1865. Use at least one example of a right that was taken away and one way freed Texans showed perseverance.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Gives an accurate setback (e.g., Black Codes of 1866, unfair labor contracts, violence) AND an accurate act of perseverance (e.g., Freedmen's Bureau schools, churches, reuniting families, freedom colonies, voting and serving in office). 1 pt — Gives only one of the two. 0 pts — No accurate example.]

15. Explain the origin and significance of Juneteenth. Why do Texans and Americans still remember June 19?
[Rubric: 2 pts — States what happened on June 19, 1865, in Galveston AND explains why it still matters (end of slavery in Texas, remembering the delay, celebrating freedom and perseverance, state holiday 1980 / national holiday 2021). 1 pt — States the origin OR the significance, but not both. 0 pts — Inaccurate or no explanation.]
