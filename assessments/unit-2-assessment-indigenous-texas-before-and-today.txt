CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 2 Assessment — Indigenous Texas Before and Today
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.1A–D · 4.9A · 4.12A · 4.19B,E · 4.20A
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. Which list names the four cultural regions of American Indian groups in Texas?
*a) Gulf, Plains, Puebloan, and Southeastern
b) Coastal Plains, North Central Plains, Great Plains, and Mountains and Basins
c) Spanish, Mexican, Republic, and State
d) North, South, East, and West

2. What shaped the four cultural regions of Texas?
a) Spanish laws
*b) Geography: land, climate, water, plants, and animals
c) Railroads
d) The Texas Constitution

3. Which cultural region had marshes, bays, and plentiful seafood?
*a) Gulf
b) Plains
c) Puebloan
d) Southeastern

4. Which group lived along the Gulf Coast?
a) Caddo
*b) Karankawa
c) Lipan Apache
d) Jumano

5. Why were the Lipan Apache nomadic?
a) They disliked staying in one place.
*b) They followed the bison herds on the Plains, which were their main source of food and supplies.
c) They were forced to move by the railroad.
d) They were traders who lived on ships.

6. What made the Caddo different from the Lipan Apache?
*a) The Caddo farmed in permanent villages in the Southeastern forests.
b) The Caddo followed bison herds across the Plains.
c) The Caddo lived only on the Gulf Coast.
d) The Caddo did not grow any crops.

7. The Caddo grew corn, beans, and squash together. What were these crops called?
a) The Cattle Trail
*b) The Three Sisters
c) The Lone Star crops
d) The Gulf harvest

8. The Jumano are best known as...
a) builders of the Alamo
*b) long-distance traders who connected groups across hundreds of miles
c) ranchers in the Panhandle
d) Spanish missionaries

9. What does "barter" mean?
*a) Trading goods directly for other goods, without money
b) Building a mound village
c) Hunting bison
d) Writing a treaty

10. Which Indigenous community is located near El Paso in far west Texas?
a) Alabama-Coushatta Tribe
b) Kickapoo Traditional Tribe
*c) Ysleta del Sur Pueblo
d) Karankawa

11. How did the Caddo and the Comanche govern themselves?
a) Both had a king chosen by Spain.
*b) The settled Caddo formed a confederacy with councils; the mobile Comanche used flexible band leadership.
c) Neither group had any leaders.
d) Both used the Texas Legislature.

12. Why is it important to study American Indian peoples as living communities, not only as people of the past?
a) Because they all moved away from Texas
*b) Because nations like the Alabama-Coushatta, Kickapoo, and Ysleta del Sur Pueblo live in Texas today with their own governments
c) Because history only matters today
d) Because they have no history before 1900

SHORT ANSWER

13. Choose two groups (Caddo, Karankawa, Lipan Apache, or Jumano). Compare how geography shaped the way each group met its needs.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Accurately names each group's region and a way of meeting needs tied to geography (e.g., Caddo farming in rich Southeastern soil; Karankawa fishing on the Gulf Coast; Lipan Apache hunting bison on the Plains; Jumano trading across regions) AND makes a comparison. 1 pt — Accurate for one group, or two groups without a comparison. 0 pts — Inaccurate.]

14. Explain why Caddo and Comanche governance were different. Is one "better" than the other? Explain.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Explains that the Caddo's settled farming life fit councils/confederacy and the Comanche's mobile plains life fit flexible band leadership, AND explains that both fit their people and land (neither is simply "better"). 1 pt — Describes the difference but not the reason, or the reason without the comparison. 0 pts — Inaccurate.]

15. Name the three federally recognized American Indian nations in Texas today and where each is located. Write one sentence about why using respectful, accurate language matters when studying them.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names all three with accurate locations (Ysleta del Sur Pueblo near El Paso; Alabama-Coushatta in East Texas near Livingston; Kickapoo Traditional Tribe near Eagle Pass) AND gives a respectful-language reason (living nations, honors their history, avoids stereotypes). 1 pt — Two or three nations without locations or reason, or the reason with one or two nations. 0 pts — Inaccurate.]
