CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 8 Assessment — Cattle, Railroads, and the Changing Frontier
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.4B,C,D · 4.7B · 4.8A,B,C · 4.10C · 4.11A,B,C · 4.19B,D,F
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. Why did Texans drive cattle north after the Civil War?
a) Texas had run out of grass.
*b) Cattle worth about $4 in Texas could sell for about $40 in the North.
c) The law required all cattle to be sold in Kansas.
d) Cattle could not live in Texas winters.

2. What is a railhead?
a) The front car of a train
b) A ranch house near the tracks
*c) The end of a railroad line, where cattle were loaded onto trains
d) A bridge over a river

3. Which leader opened a trail west to New Mexico with Oliver Loving and is said to have invented the chuck wagon?
a) Richard King
*b) Charles Goodnight
c) Quanah Parker
d) B. B. Paddock

4. Many of the first Texas cowboys learned their skills from...
*a) Tejano vaqueros.
b) railroad engineers.
c) soldiers at the forts.
d) steamboat captains.

5. The Kansas Pacific Railway printed a map calling its route "the best and shortest cattle trail from Texas." Why should a historian check this map against other evidence?
a) Old maps are always wrong.
*b) The railroad wanted cattle business, so the map was trying to persuade.
c) The map was drawn by a cowboy.
d) Maps cannot show trails.

6. Which two changes helped end the long cattle drives by the late 1880s?
a) Steamboats and canals
*b) Barbed-wire fences and railroads built in Texas
c) Gold mines and new forts
d) Telephones and cars

7. How did the state of Texas encourage companies to build railroads after the Civil War?
a) It built every railroad itself.
b) It banned wagons on public roads.
*c) It gave railroad companies land for every mile of track they built.
d) It paid every passenger to ride.

8. Which town was CREATED by a railroad company?
a) San Antonio
*b) Denison
c) Jefferson
d) Nacogdoches

9. Look at this data: Dallas had about 3,000 people in 1870, 10,358 in 1880, and 38,067 in 1890. What does the data show?
a) Dallas got smaller after the railroads arrived.
*b) Dallas grew fastest after the railroads arrived in 1872 and 1873.
c) Dallas never had a railroad.
d) Dallas stayed the same size.

10. Why were bison so important to the Comanche and Kiowa?
a) They pulled wagons to market.
*b) They gave food, robes, tipi covers, and tools.
c) They were raised on ranches for sale.
d) They were only hunted for sport.

11. Hornaday's 1889 map shows that...
a) bison were spreading into new areas of Texas.
b) cattle trails ran through Palo Duro Canyon.
*c) the land where bison lived shrank quickly, and the southern herd was gone by about the late 1870s.
d) railroads were built where bison lived.

12. Which sentence is BALANCED?
a) The forts made Texas safe.
b) Nothing good came from settling the plains.
*c) Ranchers gained grassland, while the Comanche lost the bison they depended on.
d) The plains were empty land waiting for settlers.

SHORT ANSWER

13. Did railroads follow growing towns, create growing towns, or both? Give your answer and support it with at least one town example and one piece of data.
[Rubric: 2 pts — States follow, create, or both AND supports it with an accurate town example (e.g., Dallas, Fort Worth, Denison, Abilene, Jefferson) AND at least one accurate number or data pattern (e.g., track grew from about 500 to about 8,700 miles, 1870–1890; Dallas or Fort Worth population growth). 1 pt — Gives an answer with a town example OR data, but not both. 0 pts — No answer or no accurate evidence.]

14. Choose one group from the cattle industry (for example: Tejano vaqueros, Black cowboys, women ranchers like Lizzie Johnson, ranch owners like Richard King or Charles Goodnight). Explain how that group helped the cattle industry grow, and name one risk or cost of the cattle business.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Accurately explains one group's contribution AND names an accurate risk or cost (e.g., stampedes, river crossings, storms, low prices, the long distance to market). 1 pt — Gives only one of the two. 0 pts — No accurate group or risk.]

15. How should historians describe expansion when one group's opportunity meant another group's loss? Describe one benefit of the changes on the plains and one harmful consequence, and explain how the Comanche or Kiowa saw these changes. Use fair and accurate words.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names an accurate benefit and who gained it (e.g., land for settlers and ranchers, railroad towns, jobs) AND an accurate harmful consequence (e.g., loss of the bison, Red River War, moving to reservations in Indian Territory) AND accurately represents an American Indian perspective with respectful language. 1 pt — Gives a benefit and a loss but no accurate American Indian perspective, or the response is one-sided. 0 pts — Inaccurate, one-sided, or disrespectful.]
