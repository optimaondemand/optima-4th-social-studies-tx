CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 4 Assessment — Mexican Texas and Colonization
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.3C,D · 4.4A,B · 4.7A · 4.11A,B · 4.17A,B
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. What was an empresario?
*a) A person who received a land grant from Mexico and agreed to recruit settlers to Texas.
b) A Spanish military officer who built forts in Texas.
c) A Mexican president who opposed immigration to Texas.
d) A French trader who smuggled goods into Texas.

2. Who were the two most prominent empresarios in Texas?
a) Sam Houston and Davy Crockett
*b) Stephen F. Austin and Martín de León
c) Santa Anna and Iturbide
d) William Travis and Jim Bowie

3. What were settlers required to do under the empresario system?
a) Speak only English and follow American laws.
*b) Become Mexican citizens, convert to Catholicism, and be loyal to Mexico.
c) Pay Spain a yearly tax on their land.
d) Serve in the Mexican military for five years.

4. Why did settlers choose to build colonies near rivers?
a) Rivers were the only places Mexico allowed settlement.
*b) Rivers provided water for farming, transportation, and drinking water for families and livestock.
c) Rivers were the easiest places to build forts.
d) Mexico required all settlements to be on the coast.

5. What was Stephen F. Austin's colony capital?
a) San Antonio
*b) San Felipe de Austin
c) Nacogdoches
d) Galveston

6. What crops and goods were most important to the early Texas economy?
a) Gold and silver
*b) Cotton, cattle, and corn
c) Tobacco and rice
d) Lumber and furs

7. What did the Law of April 6, 1830 do?
a) It granted more land to American settlers.
*b) It banned further American immigration to Texas and imposed new customs duties.
c) It made Texas an independent state within Mexico.
d) It gave empresarios more power over their colonies.

8. Why was the Mexican government concerned about American settlers in Texas?
a) Americans refused to farm the land.
*b) Americans outnumbered Mexican citizens in Texas and maintained American customs and language.
c) Americans were allied with France against Mexico.
d) Americans refused to pay for their land grants.

9. What was the Fredonian Rebellion?
*a) An early, failed attempt by settlers near Nacogdoches to declare independence from Mexico.
b) A battle between empresarios Austin and de León.
c) A Mexican military invasion of Texas.
d) A trade dispute between Texas and Louisiana.

10. What happened to Stephen F. Austin in Mexico City in 1834?
a) He was elected president of Mexico.
b) He signed a new empresario contract.
*c) He was arrested and imprisoned for over a year.
d) He negotiated Texas independence peacefully.

11. What were the Conventions of 1832 and 1833?
a) Military battles between Texians and Mexican troops.
*b) Meetings where Texian settlers listed their grievances and requested reforms from Mexico.
c) Celebrations of Mexican independence held in Texas.
d) Trade agreements between Texas and the United States.

12. How did free enterprise work differently in early Texas compared to what Mexico wanted?
a) Texas settlers wanted more government control of trade.
*b) Settlers wanted to trade freely with the U.S. and other countries, but Mexico restricted trade to protect its own economy.
c) Mexico encouraged unlimited free trade with all nations.
d) Settlers refused to engage in any trade at all.

SHORT ANSWER

13. Compare the colonies of Stephen F. Austin and Martín de León. How were they similar, and how were they different?
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names at least one similarity (both empresarios, recruited settlers, established towns) AND one difference (Austin's was Anglo-American/English-speaking, de León's was Mexican/Spanish-speaking; different locations). 1 pt — Identifies similarity or difference but not both. 0 pts — No specific comparison.]

14. Explain at least two causes of growing tensions between Texian settlers and the Mexican government before the Texas Revolution.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names two specific causes (Law of April 6 1830, customs disputes, cultural clashes, Fredonian Rebellion, Anahuac disturbances, Austin's arrest) with brief explanation of each. 1 pt — Names one cause with explanation. 0 pts — Vague or no specific causes.]

15. Imagine you are a Texian settler in 1834. Would you argue that Texas should stay part of Mexico or push for independence? Explain your reasoning using at least two pieces of evidence from the unit.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Takes a clear position AND supports it with two specific pieces of evidence from the lessons. 1 pt — Takes a position with only one piece of evidence. 0 pts — No position or no evidence.]
