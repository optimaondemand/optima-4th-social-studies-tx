CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 3 Assessment — Spanish Colonial Texas
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.3A,B · 4.17A · 4.19B,E
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. How was the Spanish colonial government in Texas organized?
a) Local leaders made all their own decisions independently.
*b) The King of Spain held ultimate authority, with power flowing down through the viceroy, governor, and local officials.
c) Indigenous leaders governed alongside Spanish officials as equal partners.
d) Colonists elected their own governors through popular vote.

2. What was the role of the Viceroy of New Spain?
a) He commanded the Spanish navy.
*b) He served as the king's representative in the Americas, based in Mexico City.
c) He was the local governor of Texas.
d) He led the missions and converted Indigenous peoples.

3. What was the encomienda system?
a) A system for electing colonial leaders.
b) A trade agreement between Spain and France.
*c) A system that gave Spanish settlers the right to demand labor and tribute from Indigenous peoples.
d) A method of building missions throughout Texas.

4. How did Spanish trade restrictions affect colonists in Texas?
*a) Colonists could only trade with Spain or other Spanish colonies, limiting goods and raising prices.
b) Colonists could trade freely with anyone.
c) All trade was completely banned.
d) Colonists were encouraged to trade with France and England.

5. Which group was most harmed by Spanish colonial policies?
a) Spanish-born officials (peninsulares)
b) Criollos (American-born Spanish)
*c) Indigenous peoples
d) French traders

6. What was the Grito de Dolores?
a) A Spanish law banning colonial trade.
*b) Father Miguel Hidalgo's call for Mexican independence in 1810.
c) A treaty between Spain and France over Texas.
d) The name of the first Spanish mission in Texas.

7. Who led the final march into Mexico City that completed Mexican independence in 1821?
a) Miguel Hidalgo
b) José María Morelos
*c) Agustín de Iturbide
d) Stephen F. Austin

8. What changed for Texas when Mexico became independent from Spain in 1821?
a) Nothing changed — Spain still controlled Texas.
*b) Texas became part of the new nation of Mexico, with new laws and policies.
c) Texas immediately became its own independent country.
d) Texas was given to France.

9. How did mission life affect Indigenous peoples' daily routines?
*a) The mission system regulated nearly every aspect of daily life, including when to wake, pray, work, and eat.
b) Indigenous peoples were free to follow their own traditions inside the missions.
c) Missions only required attendance at Sunday services.
d) Indigenous peoples ran the missions themselves.

10. What eventually resulted from the unequal effects of Spanish colonial policies?
a) Everyone became satisfied with the system.
b) Spain gave Indigenous peoples more power.
*c) Resentment grew among criollos and Indigenous peoples, contributing to independence movements.
d) Spain abandoned all its colonies voluntarily.

11. What is the correct order of authority in Spanish colonial government?
*a) King → Council of the Indies → Viceroy → Governor → Local Officials
b) Governor → Viceroy → King → Council of the Indies
c) Viceroy → King → Governor → Local Officials
d) Council of the Indies → King → Viceroy → Governor

12. How did Texas change under Mexican rule compared to Spanish rule?
a) Trade became even more restricted.
*b) Mexico opened Texas to more immigration and offered land grants to attract settlers.
c) The mission system was expanded with more missions built.
d) Indigenous peoples gained full control of the government.

SHORT ANSWER

13. Describe two ways that Spanish colonial policies affected different groups of people in Texas. Use specific examples from what you've learned.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names two policies (e.g., encomienda, trade restrictions, mission system, land grants) AND explains how each affected a specific group. 1 pt — Names one policy with explanation. 0 pts — Vague or no specific examples.]

14. Explain why Mexico wanted independence from Spain. What were the main causes, and who were the key leaders?
[Rubric: 2 pts — Identifies at least two causes (inequality between peninsulares/criollos, lack of self-governance, Enlightenment ideas) AND names at least one leader (Hidalgo, Morelos, or Iturbide) with their role. 1 pt — Identifies one cause or one leader with partial explanation. 0 pts — No specific causes or leaders mentioned.]

15. Compare life in Texas under Spanish rule with life under early Mexican rule. What stayed the same? What changed?
[Rubric: 2 pts — Identifies at least one continuity AND one change with specific details. 1 pt — Identifies either a continuity or a change but not both. 0 pts — No specific comparison made.]
