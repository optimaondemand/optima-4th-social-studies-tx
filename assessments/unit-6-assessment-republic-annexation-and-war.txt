CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 6 Assessment — Republic, Annexation, and War
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.3C · 4.3D · 4.3E · 4.13A,B · 4.14A,C · 4.16B · 4.19B,E,G
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. The Constitution of the Republic of Texas (1836) divided the government into three branches. What were they?
a) Army, navy, and Texas Rangers
*b) Legislative, executive, and judicial
c) Counties, towns, and missions
d) President, governor, and mayor

2. Which group did the Constitution of 1836 leave out of citizenship?
a) Settlers who came from the United States
b) People born in San Antonio
*c) American Indians and people of African descent
d) Members of Congress

3. What were "redbacks"?
a) Uniforms worn by the Texas Rangers
*b) Paper money printed by the Republic that quickly lost value because people did not trust it
c) A nickname for Mexican soldiers
d) Treaties signed with the Cherokee

4. How did President Sam Houston try to deal with American Indian nations?
*a) He made peace treaties and met with their leaders.
b) He sent the army to force all of them out of Texas.
c) He ignored them.
d) He asked Mexico to handle them.

5. Which president moved the capital to Austin and set aside public land to pay for schools?
a) Sam Houston
b) Anson Jones
c) David G. Burnet
*d) Mirabeau B. Lamar

6. Who was the Tejano leader who signed the Texas Declaration of Independence, served in the Republic's Congress, and protected Tejano voting rights at the 1845 convention?
a) Lorenzo de Zavala
*b) José Antonio Navarro
c) Juan Seguín
d) Martín de León

7. Under the Constitution of 1836, what did the president need before a treaty could take effect?
a) A vote of every Texan
b) Permission from the Supreme Court
*c) The approval of two-thirds of the Senate
d) Permission from the United States

8. What happened to Sam Houston's 1836 treaty with the Cherokee?
a) It was approved and the Cherokee kept their land.
*b) The Texas Senate refused to approve it, and the Cherokee were later forced out of Texas.
c) Mexico canceled it.
d) It was never written down.

9. What did President Anson Jones do in 1845 when Texas had two offers: join the United States, or peace and independence with Mexico?
a) He chose for Texas without asking anyone.
b) He tore up both offers.
*c) He put both choices before the Texas Congress and a convention of delegates.
d) He asked Great Britain to decide.

10. On what date did Texas become the 28th state of the United States?
a) March 2, 1836
b) April 21, 1836
*c) December 29, 1845
d) February 2, 1848

11. What was the main cause of the U.S.-Mexican War?
*a) The United States and Mexico disagreed about the border: the Rio Grande or the Nueces River.
b) Mexico wanted to take back the Alamo.
c) Texas refused to join the United States.
d) A trade argument over cotton.

12. What does the Pledge to the Texas Flag mean when it calls Texas "one and indivisible"?
a) Texas has only one city.
*b) Texas is united and cannot be split apart.
c) Texas has one river.
d) Texas will always be its own country.

SHORT ANSWER

13. Choose one problem the Republic of Texas faced (debt, defense, recognition from other countries, American Indian relations, or frontier safety). Explain what the problem was and what leaders tried to do about it.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names a specific problem AND accurately describes what leaders tried (e.g., debt → printed paper money/redbacks; recognition → U.S. recognized Texas in 1837; American Indian relations → Houston's treaties vs. Lamar's war; frontier → Texas Rangers). 1 pt — Names a problem with a vague or partly inaccurate response. 0 pts — No specific problem.]

14. Can a leader make a responsible decision that still turns out badly? Use one decision from Unit 6 (for example, Houston's Cherokee treaty, Lamar's Santa Fe Expedition, or Jones's two offers) to explain your answer.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Takes a clear position AND uses a specific Unit 6 decision, separating the reasoning (evidence, Constitution, people affected) from the result. 1 pt — Takes a position with a general example, or describes the decision without judging reasoning vs. result. 0 pts — No position or no example.]

15. Was annexation the wisest path for Texas in 1845? Write a claim, give one piece of evidence, name one consequence of annexation, and answer one counterpoint.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Clear claim + accurate evidence + a real consequence (e.g., war with Mexico, Rio Grande border, Mexican Cession, $10 million in 1850 for the Republic's debt) + a counterpoint answered respectfully. 1 pt — Claim with evidence or consequence but no counterpoint. 0 pts — No claim or no supporting detail.]
