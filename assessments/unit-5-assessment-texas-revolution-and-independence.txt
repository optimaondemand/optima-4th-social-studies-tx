Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.3D · 4.4A · 4.7A · 4.14A · 4.17A,B · 4.19B,C

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. What was the significance of the Battle of Gonzales in 1835?
a) It was the final battle of the Texas Revolution.
*b) It was the first military conflict of the Texas Revolution, where Texians refused to return a cannon to Mexico.
c) It was where the Texas Declaration of Independence was signed.
d) It was where Santa Anna was captured.

2. What phrase appeared on the flag flown at the Battle of Gonzales?
a) "Remember the Alamo"
b) "Don't Tread on Me"
*c) "Come and Take It"

a) San Antonio

a) 13

SHORT ANSWER

13. Explain the difference between what actually happened at the Alamo and how the event has been remembered over time. Why is it important for historians to separate event from memory?
[Rubric: 2 pts — Identifies at least one historical fact about the Alamo AND one way it has been mythologized or remembered differently, PLUS explains why distinguishing event from memory matters. 1 pt — Identifies fact or myth but not both, or no explanation of why it matters. 0 pts — No specific examples.]

14. Trace the path from the Battle of Gonzales to the Battle of San Jacinto. Name at least three key events in order and explain how each one led to the next.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names 3+ events in correct chronological order (Gonzales, Consultation, Alamo, Goliad, Runaway Scrape, San Jacinto) with cause-effect connections. 1 pt — Names events but out of order or without connections. 0 pts — Fewer than 2 events or no sequence.]

15. The Republic of Texas faced many challenges after winning independence. Name at least two challenges and explain why each one was difficult for the new nation.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names 2+ challenges (debt, border disputes with Mexico, lack of international recognition, Indigenous relations, small population/military) with explanation. 1 pt — Names one challenge with explanation. 0 pts — No specific challenges.]
