CANVAS QUIZ: Unit 11 Assessment — Texas Government, Citizenship, and Economy
Course: Grade 4 Texas Social Studies
Standards: 4.10A–B · 4.11A–D · 4.13A–B · 4.15A–F · 4.16A–B · 4.22A–B
Points per question: 1
Question count: 15 (12 multiple choice + 3 short answer)

---

MULTIPLE CHOICE

1. The Texas Constitution says, "All political power is inherent in the people." What does this mean?
a) The governor holds all the power.
*b) The government's power comes from the people of Texas.
c) Only judges have power.
d) Power comes from the federal government only.

2. When did Texas voters approve the constitution Texas uses today?
a) 1836
b) 1845
*c) 1876
d) 1976

3. Which branch of Texas government MAKES laws?
*a) Legislative
b) Executive
c) Judicial
d) The county fair

4. Who leads the executive branch of Texas and can sign or veto bills?
a) The lieutenant governor
*b) The governor
c) The Speaker of the House
d) The chief justice

5. Someone says a new law breaks the Texas Constitution. Which branch decides?
a) Legislative
b) Executive
*c) Judicial
d) No one can decide.

6. Why does Texas divide government power among three branches?
a) So laws can never be passed
*b) So no single leader holds all the power, which protects people's rights
c) Because the Capitol has three wings
d) So the governor can make every decision

7. Which of these must a person be to vote in Texas?
a) At least 16 years old
*b) A U.S. citizen, at least 18, a Texas resident, and registered
c) A member of the Legislature
d) A business owner

8. Which action can a fourth grader take right now to participate in their community?
a) Vote in a city election
*b) Write a respectful letter to a city council member
c) Sign bills into law
d) Serve as a judge

9. Adina De Zavala is remembered for...
a) inventing barbed wire.
*b) working to preserve the Alamo and other Texas historic places.
c) leading the Texas Senate.
d) building the Houston Ship Channel.

10. Which comment at a public meeting shows respectful civil discourse?
a) "Anyone who disagrees with me is silly."
*b) "I disagree because the building is over 100 years old. Here is a photo from 1925."
c) Talking loudly while someone else is speaking
d) Leaving before anyone else can speak

11. A dry summer means fewer tomatoes grow, but people still want to buy them. What usually happens to the price of tomatoes?
*a) It goes up.
b) It goes down.
c) It stays exactly the same.
d) Tomatoes become free.

12. A company chooses to build a factory on the Gulf Coast instead of in the Panhandle. The Panhandle's cheaper land is the company's...
a) supply
b) demand
*c) opportunity cost
d) export

SHORT ANSWER

13. Choose one scenario and explain which branch of Texas government handles it and why: (a) lawmakers want to create a new school-safety law; (b) the governor decides whether to sign a bill; (c) two businesses disagree about what a law means. Then give one example of checks and balances.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Correctly matches the scenario to its branch (a = legislative, b = executive, c = judicial) with a reason based on that branch's job AND gives an accurate check (veto, two-thirds override, courts ruling on the constitution, Senate approving appointments). 1 pt — Correct branch with reason, or an accurate check, but not both. 0 pts — Inaccurate.]

14. What does responsible participation require before, during, and after a public decision? Name one action for each stage.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Gives an accurate action for all three stages (before: learn/research; during: speak respectfully, listen, vote if eligible; after: accept a fair result, keep serving, hold leaders accountable). 1 pt — Accurate actions for two stages. 0 pts — One or none.]

15. How can an economic choice benefit buyers or businesses while still creating a cost for someone else? Use one example from a Texas region.
[Rubric: 2 pts — Names a specific choice and region (e.g., factory near the Port of Houston, new highway bringing Rio Grande Valley produce) AND explains both a benefit (jobs, lower prices, trade) and a cost to someone else (traffic, a local seller losing business, another town losing the factory). 1 pt — Gives a benefit and a cost without a regional example, or a regional example with only one side. 0 pts — No accurate explanation.]
