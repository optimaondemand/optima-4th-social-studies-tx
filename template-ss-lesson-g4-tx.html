Word Roots
✏️ Latin Root: terra — "land, earth"

The Latin root terra means "land" or "earth." You'll find it in many social studies words.

📖

territory — an area of land · terrain — the physical features of land · terrestrial — relating to the earth · Mediterranean — "middle of the land"

🔎 Discovery Fact
✏️ The word "Mediterranean" literally means "middle of the land" — the ancient Romans thought their sea was at the center of the world!
